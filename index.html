What is KASDIT ?


In a world where digital innovation is constantly evolving, Kasaudit emerged as a specialized force within the Web3 landscape, solely dedicated to fortifying the KASPA blockchain. Founded on the principle that trust is the foundation of any technology, Kasaudit stands as the gatekeeper, ensuring that each transaction, contract, and code line within the KASPA network is secure, transparent, and resilient.






Project Execution
Inspired by the strengths of pioneers like Certik and Solidproof, Kasaudit’s purpose is clear yet unique: to bring unparaleled scrutiny and assurance to a single blockchain ecosystem. By focusing exclusively on KASPA, Kasaudit offers its community a level of expertise and dedication unmatched by generalized audit firms.


Project Execution
Every protocol, token, and decentralized application within KASPA’s ecosystem is meticulously examined by Kasaudit’s team of experts, ensuring they are free from vulnerabilities and built to withstand the chalenges of an ever-evolving Web3 environment

About Kasdit

Our core team, led by CEO Mails Nielsen and CFO Kevin Arens consists of highly skilled developers with diverse backgrounds. Leveraging our combined expertise from the University of Flensburg and industry experience, we possess an exceptional understanding of security protocols and best practices, ensuring the highest standards of professionalism and safeguarding our clients' interests.

Based in Germany, we operate proficiently in multiple languages, including technical, German, English, Turkish, Swedish, Norwegian, Chinese, and Indian.
